# PDF dependencies

Loaded only when a PDF is requested; all files are served from this repository.

- pdf-lib 1.17.1, MIT: https://github.com/Hopding/pdf-lib . License in vendor/pdf-lib-LICENSE.md.
- @pdf-lib/fontkit 1.1.1, MIT: https://github.com/Hopding/fontkit . Upstream publishes its license declaration in README.md and package.json, bundled here as vendor/fontkit-README.md and vendor/fontkit-package.json.
- DejaVu Sans, Latin subset: https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/ . Upstream font copyright and license in fonts/LICENSE.txt. The subset retains the original family name and copyright metadata.

Downloaded distributions: https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/pdf-lib@1.17.1/dist/pdf-lib.min.js and https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@pdf-lib/fontkit@1.1.1/dist/fontkit.umd.min.js.
